BetaBuddy Privacy Policy
Updated: November 28, 2026

Your climbing stays on your device.

BetaBuddy is a climbing video review logbook and gym route viewer that runs locally on your iPhone. Core video review and gym features are free. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks additional gyms, cat climbing and a permanent lifetime member badge. There are no subscriptions, ads, or accounts.

1. What the app handles
Videos you choose are copied into the app for on-device pose estimation. The app stores the video copy, a thumbnail, pose keypoints, analysis results, the grade and notes you enter, and whether you marked the climb as sent. None of this is sent to the developer or to any third-party server, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK that collects usage.

Import saves the original video without pose analysis by default. The app reads the selected video’s recording time (or the selected media item’s date), stores it as the editable climb time, and uses the current time when it is unavailable. Only analysis you request runs in the background on your device. Unfinished requests are kept locally and retried when you return to the app.

When you use “Analyse this attempt”, the app takes a set of still frames from the clip and hands them, together with the pose data, to the analysis page that ships inside the app, where the work is done on the device. The frames are used in memory only and are not saved. The app stores the result for that climb locally (it includes an image of the wall composed from the video frames), along with the body measurements, such as height and weight, that you set on the analysis page. The app also keeps, locally, the climbing style the analysis page worked out (bouldering, top rope or lead; you can change it) and an edit plan. Like the Gym page, the analysis page cannot reach the network, and none of this leaves the device.

When you use “Auto edit”, the app makes a new video on the device from that edit plan. The edited video is a temporary file, deleted when you close the page or delete the record, and is not written to your photo library; it is handed to the app or recipient you pick only when you tap “Share video” yourself.

When you record inside the app, it uses the back camera to record video. It does not record sound and does not use the microphone. While you frame the shot, the app hands scaled-down preview frames, the phone's tilt and movement and, when the device can keep up, body keypoints found in those frames to the capture page that ships inside the app, which checks the camera position and recognises holds on the device. These frames are used in memory only and are not saved one by one. When recording starts, the page produces a capture plan that contains a still picture of the wall made from the preview frames. When you stop, the recorded video is stored in the app's local directory together with the gym, grade and colour you chose and that capture plan, including the picture of the wall; they are deleted when you delete the record; pose recognition and the analysis of that attempt then run on the device. Like the Gym page, the capture page cannot reach the network. Camera frames and recordings do not leave the device unless you share an edited video yourself through the system share sheet.

If you allow location access, the app asks the system for your location once on the capture screen, to order the gym directory that ships inside the app by distance, on the device. The location is not saved and does not leave the device. Without it, recording and choosing a gym work as before.

When choosing a climber, the app detects candidate people on the device and keeps poses, coarse clothing-colour samples, and the times and positions you select for tracking and correction within that video. It does not perform face recognition or identify people across videos. Original person crops are not retained and the background is not changed. Choosing another person invalidates the previous analysis and edit. Deleting the record also deletes its person data.

2. Model and network
The pose model ships inside the app and runs on the device. The app does not download models and does not call any cloud AI or large language model. If you pick a video that exists only in iCloud, the system may download the original first; that is part of Apple's Photos service. App Store downloads, purchases, restore transactions, system diagnostics and system services follow Apple's settings and policies. Apple handles payment details; BetaBuddy checks the purchase entitlement with StoreKit on the device.

3. Gym
The gym model, routes and climbing simulation in the Gym tab ship inside the app and open on the device. That page cannot reach the network. The app does not record or send which routes you look at or which body parameters you choose there. The gym content is an unofficial compilation and may not match the gym as it is today; the simulation is a model's projection, not coaching advice.

4. Photos and sharing
You can manually import videos through the system photo picker. Only after you enable automatic import and grant permission does the app check allowed videos while open. A bundled offline model identifies climbing and indoor or outdoor settings, and matching videos are copied into your journal. Limited access is supported; turning the feature off or revoking permission stops checking. Automatic import does not download cloud originals; download them in Photos first to retry. Filming time and location already stored with each video are used locally to group by day and suggest a gym. Current device location is not used to guess where old videos were filmed, and precise coordinates are not stored. You can correct the environment and gym. Local photo asset identifiers and processing receipts are retained for incremental checking and deduplication, including after deleting a journal record so it is not automatically imported again. These receipts are removed when the app is deleted. The photo library originals are not modified. When you use the system share sheet, the text or the edited video you choose is sent to the app or recipient you pick and is then covered by their privacy policy.

5. Storage and deletion
Records are stored in the app's local directory, which is excluded from system backups. You can delete the video copy and analysis of any single record; for a record made with the in-app camera its capture plan is deleted with it. Deleting the app removes its local data. Original videos in your photo library are not affected. Copies you exported must be deleted wherever you saved them. The app has no cloud restore.

6. Changes and contact
If the way data is handled changes, this policy will be updated with the app. Privacy and support questions: hi@dozycat.ai.
