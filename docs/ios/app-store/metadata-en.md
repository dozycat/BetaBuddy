## Promotional text

Explore an offline gym and replay your climbs with on-device pose analysis. Core Gym and Logbook features are free; lifetime membership is an optional one-time purchase.

## Description

Every go teaches you something.

BetaBuddy has two tabs: Gym and Logbook.

GYM
• An unofficial 3D map of one climbing gym, built into the app. Filter problems by wall, grade band and hold color, and see where each one starts and finishes.
• Different build, same problem: pick a climber’s build and step through one sequence the model projects for it.
• Hold positions and problems were compiled by climbers from public material and may not match the wall. The projection is not coaching advice. Trust what you see at the gym and your own judgment.

LOGBOOK
• Import and save your own climbing video before pose analysis. Choose whether to analyze after saving; 33 body keypoints are found on your phone and replayed over the original footage.
• Two analysis modes: Everyday or Close look.
• Step 0.1 seconds at a time, slow to 0.5×, and jump straight to cues worth another look.
• Note the grade, gym, climbing date and time, whether you sent, and one thing to try differently next go.
• Organize videos in folders and combine gym, grade, date and sent-status filters. Search by name, grade or gym.
• Choose a gym from the built-in offline directory or enter your own. Directory coverage is partial; it does not verify current opening status.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account. No video upload. No cloud AI. The pose model and the gym data ship inside the app, and the gym page cannot go online.
• Gym and Logbook core features are free, with no ads or subscriptions. An optional one-time lifetime membership immediately adds a permanent badge to the Logbook.

Works with videos of one climber, whole body in frame, from a fixed camera: 0.5 seconds–20 minutes, up to 500 MB each. Trim anything else in Photos first.

Pose results are an aid for review. Occlusion, other people in frame and camera movement affect detection. 2D angles and the hip trail do not describe full 3D body mechanics. Judge against the original video.

Requires iOS 17 or later. If a video exists only in iCloud, the system may need to download the original first. Local records are not restored from system backups; share out any notes you want to keep.

The app’s interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It follows your system language, and you can switch it in the app at any time.

## What’s New (1.4.0)

Save your climbing video immediately, then choose whether to analyze its skeleton. You can leave the review while analysis runs; if iOS stops background work, it resumes when you return. Recording time is read from the video when available and can be edited. Videos up to 20 minutes are supported, subject to the 500 MB file limit.
