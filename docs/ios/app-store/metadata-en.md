## Description

Every go teaches you something.

BetaBuddy has two tabs: Gym and Logbook.

GYM
• An unofficial 3D map of one climbing gym, built into the app. Filter problems by wall, grade band and hold color, and see where each one starts and finishes.
• Different build, same problem: pick a climber’s build and step through one sequence the model projects for it.
• Hold positions and problems were compiled by climbers from public material and may not match the wall. The projection is not coaching advice. Trust what you see at the gym and your own judgment.

LOGBOOK
• Import your own climbing video. 33 body keypoints are found on your phone, and you replay the skeleton over the original footage.
• Two analysis modes: Everyday or Close look.
• Step 0.1 seconds at a time, slow to 0.5×, and jump straight to cues worth another look.
• Note the grade, whether you sent, and one thing to try differently next go. Filter by sent or projecting; search by name or grade.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account. No video upload. No cloud AI. The pose model and the gym data ship inside the app, and the gym page cannot go online.
• Everything is free: no ads, no purchases, no subscriptions.

Works with videos of one climber, whole body in frame, from a fixed camera: 0.5–180 seconds, up to 500 MB. Trim in Photos first.

Pose results are an aid for review. Occlusion, other people in frame and camera movement affect detection. 2D angles and the hip trail do not describe full 3D body mechanics. Judge against the original video.

Requires iOS 17 or later. If a video exists only in iCloud, the system may need to download the original first. Local records are not restored from system backups; share out any notes you want to keep.

The app’s interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It follows your system language, and you can switch it in the app at any time.
